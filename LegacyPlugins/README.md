Plugins in this folder are mostly for HoneySelect 1 and are no longer maintained.

The source of MoreAccessories included here is unchanged from Joan's original code. It has since been forked into [a separate repository](https://github.com/IllusionMods/MoreAccessories) and is being maintained there.

Many of these plugins may be impossible to build without extensive fixes. Feel free to submit fixes, although they will not be tested beyond the bare minimum since most devs don't have HS1.
